About Me

From the wetlands of Australia to research labs, my work revolves around understanding how mosquitoes interact with their environment and the pathogens they carry. My PhD research combines fieldwork, next-generation sequencing, and disease modelling to explore how Japanese encephalitis virus circulates in commercial pig farms. Whether in the field collecting bloodfed mosquitoes or analysing sequencing data, I am passionate about bridging the gap between science and public health.
