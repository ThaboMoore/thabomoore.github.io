About Me

I've always been drawn to the intersection of ecology, data, and infectious disease. Mosquitoes, though small, shape ecosystems, economies, and human health in profound ways. My research brings together field observations, molecular biology, and computational models to understand how Japanese encephalitis virus circulates in Australia. It's about more than just mosquitoes—it's about understanding the delicate balance between the environment, wildlife, and human health.

🧪 Core Expertise

  • Next-generation sequencing (NGS)
  • Disease transmission modelling
  • Field survey design

💻 Technical Skills
